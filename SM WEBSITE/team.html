Founder and Owner

Meet the Founder of Seza Motors

Automotive Sales, Management and Finance Experience

Ottawa raised, the founder of Seza Motors brings years of experience in automotive sales, dealership management, and vehicle financing.

His automotive career includes six years of experience with Hunt Club Nissan, where he worked as both a Sales Associate and Sales Manager. He also gained valuable financial experience working as a Finance Manager with Ford Credit.

He studied Business Administration at Algonquin College in Ottawa, building a strong foundation in customer service, business operations, and professional communication.

Seza Motors was created to give Ottawa customers a more personal vehicle-buying experience—one built around honesty, fair pricing, and straightforward advice.

Why Seza Motors Was Started

Building Trust One Customer at a Time

After years of working in automotive sales and finance, the founder of Seza Motors saw how overwhelming the vehicle buying process could feel for many customers.

Seza Motors was created to offer a different kind of dealership experience—one where customers feel heard, informed, and respected throughout the entire process.

Our goal is not simply to sell vehicles. It is to build long-term relationships and become a trusted local name for quality affordable transportation in Ottawa.
